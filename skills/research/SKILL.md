# Research

1. Define the question, constraints, and decision criteria. Inspect the project when its context affects the answer.
2. Gather current evidence from multiple credible sources. Prioritize official documentation, standards, research papers, changelogs, advisories, and source repositories.
3. Verify important claims across sources. Check dates, versions, applicability, and conflicting evidence.
4. Evaluate options by relevant factors such as technical fit, maturity, maintenance, security, licensing, operational cost, and migration risk.
5. Provide the recommendation first. Support it with concise evidence, tradeoffs, uncertainties, confidence, and direct links beside the claims they support.
